ETHITH Messenger — Privacy Policy

Package: uz.ethith.messenger · Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview

ETHITH Messenger is an independent messaging client built on the open-source Telegram MTProto API. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum data required to deliver core messaging, media, and optional personalization features.

2. Data Processed

  • Account & Messages: Your phone number, profile information, contacts (if permission is granted), messages, and media are transmitted directly to Telegram servers via encrypted MTProto connections governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.
  • Optional Cloud Preferences: If you customize ETHITH theme preferences, profile badge styles, or interactive social features, only non-sensitive preference metadata (numeric user ID and style configuration) is synchronized to restore your preferences across logins.
  • Voice-to-Text & Translation: Audio or text submitted explicitly by you for transcription or translation is processed on-demand solely to return the requested transcript or translation and is not sold or used for advertising.
  • Local Storage & Security: Chat lock PINs, hidden chat settings, and local caches are stored locally on your device.

3. Permissions

ETHITH Messenger requests standard messaging permissions (Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Storage/Media, Notifications, Location) strictly when you use the corresponding feature (voice messages, calls, sharing photos/location, or receiving notifications).

4. Data Sharing & Ads

We do not sell your personal data, do not share your messages with third-party advertisers, and do not display third-party ad trackers.

5. Data Deletion & Contact

You can log out, clear local data, or delete your account at any time from Settings. For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact us at: support@ethith.uz or via our repository at github.com/joraboymuhammad-code/ethith-messenger-updates.
